# ADR-XXXX: Title

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-YYYY
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints, evidence, and pressures
exist right now? Write it so a stranger (or you, in six months) understands
why doing nothing was not an option.

## Decision

The choice, stated in one or two sentences, in the active voice.
"We will…" — not "it might be nice to…".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are explicitly giving
up. Include the uncomfortable ones; this section is worthless if it only
lists upsides.

## Evidence needed to revisit

The specific, observable facts that would reopen this decision. This is the
section that forces intellectual honesty about the future: if you can't name
what would change your mind, you haven't made a decision — you've formed an
attachment.
